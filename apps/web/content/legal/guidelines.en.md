# Community guidelines

Contraponha exists for conversations that move the discussion forward. Disagreeing is welcome; attacking people is not. These guidelines say what we expect from those who take part and which rules moderation applies. Every removal cites the rule used, and the affected person always receives the decision.

This is a translation. If there is any difference, the Portuguese version prevails.

## The spirit

- **Criticize the argument, not the person.** A strong contribution answers what was said, with data, questions or counterpoints.
- **Bring sources.** When you state a fact, say where it comes from. "Needs a source" is a constructive request, not an insult.
- **Change your mind in public.** Acknowledging a good argument ("Changed my mind") is part of the game.
- **Write for your readers.** Clear titles, context and calm help everyone.

## The rules

### Personal attacks {#personal_attack}

Do not insult, mock or dismiss someone for who they are instead of what they said. Examples: name-calling, "go study before giving an opinion", remarks about appearance, age or profession used to shut down the conversation.

### Harassment {#harassment}

Do not stalk, intimidate or coordinate attacks against a person, inside or outside a discussion. Repeated messages against the same person count as harassment even if each one, on its own, seems mild.

### Hate speech {#hate_speech}

Do not attack or dehumanize people based on race, color, ethnicity, origin, religion, disability, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation or other characteristics protected by Brazilian law.

### Misinformation {#misinformation}

Do not publish demonstrably false information that can cause concrete harm, such as about public health, electoral processes or emergencies. Controversial opinions and interpretations are not misinformation; when in doubt, moderation can add context with sources instead of removing.

### Spam {#spam}

Do not publish repeated advertising, misleading links, mass automated content or attempts to manipulate reactions and ranking.

### Violence {#violence}

Do not threaten, incite or celebrate violence against people or groups, and do not share violent images without journalistic or educational context.

### Sexual content {#sexual_content}

Do not publish explicit sexual content. Any sexual content involving minors is removed and reported to the authorities.

### Exposure of personal data {#privacy}

Do not publish other people's personal data without permission, such as addresses, phone numbers, documents or health information.

### Illegal content {#illegal_content}

Do not use Contraponha for activities that are illegal under Brazilian law, including selling prohibited products and copyright infringement.

### Impersonation {#impersonation}

Do not pretend to be another person, company or institution in a way that misleads readers. Parody profiles must make that clear in their name and bio.

## How moderation works

1. Anyone can report a post, a contribution or a profile. The reported person does not know who reported them.
2. A person on the team reviews the report. Algorithms help organize the queue, but do not decide alone.
3. When something is removed, the text disappears, a "removed by moderation" mark takes its place and the rule applied is shown to everyone.
4. The author receives the decision with the rule. So does the person who reported.
5. When facts are in doubt, moderation can add context above the post, always with sources.

Every moderation action is recorded in an internal log that cannot be erased, and the aggregate numbers appear in the transparency report.
