# Transparency

This page brings together how we make decisions that affect what you see and what can be published on Contraponha.

## What we publish

- **Rules.** The [community guidelines](/guidelines) list every rule moderation can apply. No removal happens without a cited rule.
- **Ranking.** How the feeds work is described in [How it works](/about): signals used, weights and what never counts.
- **Edit history.** Every earlier version of a post stays public while the post exists.

## Reports

Every six months we publish a report with:

- number of reports received, by reason;
- number of pieces of content removed, by rule applied;
- number of context notes added;
- data and removal requests made by authorities, and how many were fulfilled;
- average response time to reports.

The first report will be published at the end of the first six months of operation.

## Moderation log

Every moderation action (removal, dismissed report, context note, role change) is recorded in an append-only internal log. This log is the basis for the reports and for any review of a decision.

## Requests from authorities

We only hand over user data upon a court order or valid request, under the Civil Rights Framework for the Internet, and we notify the affected person whenever the law allows.
