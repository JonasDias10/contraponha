# How Ponto works

Ponto is a Brazilian social network built for conversations worth having. Instead of rewarding what gets the most clicks, it gives prominence to what moves the discussion forward.

## Reactions that say something

There is no "like". You react by saying what you thought: **Good argument**, **Insightful**, **Changed my mind** or **Needs a source**. Each person gives up to two reactions per post. There is no generic negative reaction: disagreeing is a written contribution.

## Two feeds, clear rules

- **Following** shows posts from people you follow, in chronological order. When you have read everything, we tell you: "You are all caught up".
- **Discover** shows posts from the last seven days, ordered by good arguments, insights, changed minds and contributions, with time weighing against older posts. Posts from topics you follow get priority, and each recommendation says why it showed up for you.

Followers, views and screen time never count.

## Contributions, not comments

In discussions, contributions are ordered by how much they move the conversation forward. The author of the post can **highlight** contributions that brought something new, such as field data or a source. Replies go at most two levels deep, so the conversation does not get lost.

## Everything in the open

- **Editing with history.** Posts can be edited during the first 30 minutes, and every version stays public, with the stated reason.
- **Moderation with a rule.** Every removal cites the rule from the [guidelines](/guidelines) that was applied, and the affected person is notified.
- **Context with sources.** When a post carries information that needs context, moderation can add it above the post, with sources.

## Calm alerts

News shows up as a dot, with no red number. You choose what is worth an alert and can receive alerts in batches, at the times you prefer.

## Your data is yours

No targeted ads. Export everything you published or delete your account whenever you want, with 30 days to change your mind. Details in the [Privacy policy](/privacy).
