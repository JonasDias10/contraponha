# Privacy policy

This is Contraponha's privacy policy, written to be read. It explains which personal data we process, why, for how long and how you exercise your rights under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, LGPD).

This is a translation. If there is any difference, the Portuguese version prevails.

## Who we are

Contraponha is a Brazilian social network. For the purposes of the LGPD, we are the controller of the personal data processed in the service. To reach the person in charge of data processing (data protection officer), write to {{dpoEmail}}.

## What we collect

| Data                                                            | What for                                                                    | Legal basis (LGPD, art. 7)                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Email, name, username and password (stored only as a hash)      | Creating and protecting your account, sending account notices               | Performance of a contract                                        |
| Date of birth                                                   | Confirming you are 16 or older; it does not appear on your profile          | Compliance with a legal obligation and performance of a contract |
| Bio, credential, location, website and pronouns                 | Showing your profile, as you choose to fill it in                           | Performance of a contract                                        |
| Posts, contributions, reactions, sources and images you publish | Working as a social network                                                 | Performance of a contract                                        |
| Who you follow and the topics you follow                        | Building your feeds                                                         | Performance of a contract                                        |
| Sessions (device and time of use)                               | Account security and ending sessions                                        | Legitimate interest                                              |
| Reports you send or receive                                     | Moderation and enforcing the guidelines                                     | Legitimate interest and legal obligation                         |
| Application access logs                                         | Required by the Brazilian Civil Rights Framework for the Internet (art. 15) | Compliance with a legal obligation                               |

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts or data from other apps. We do not use your data for ads and we do not sell it.

## How we use it

- **Feeds.** "Following" shows, in chronological order, the people you follow. "Discover" uses good argument and insightful reactions and the topics you follow. You can reset these signals at any time in Settings.
- **Notifications.** Only the ones you turn on, at the times you choose. Moderation decisions about you always arrive.
- **Moderation.** When there is a report, a person on the team sees the reported content.

## Who we share it with

We use infrastructure providers (servers, database, image storage and email delivery) that process data only on our behalf and under contract. We prioritize providers with servers in Brazil. We only share data with authorities when the law requires it, by court order or valid request.

## How long we keep it

- **Account and content:** while the account exists. Earlier versions of posts stay visible for transparency while the post exists.
- **Sessions:** until they expire or are ended; the history is available for 30 days.
- **Data exports:** the file is available for 7 days and then deleted.
- **Access logs:** 6 months, as required by the Civil Rights Framework for the Internet.
- **Moderation history:** kept for auditing, without the personal data of deleted accounts.

## Your rights

Without sending an email, you can, in **Settings › Your data**:

- **Access and take your data** (art. 18, II and V): request an export as readable HTML or JSON, with every version of your posts.
- **Correct** (art. 18, III): edit your profile at any time.
- **Delete** (art. 18, VI): delete the account. Everything is deleted in 30 days; until then, just sign in again to change your mind. Contributions to other people's discussions become "deleted account", unless you ask to delete them too.
- **Revoke consents and reset recommendations** (art. 18, IX).

For any other request, including information about sharing (art. 18, VII), write to {{dpoEmail}}. We reply within 15 days.

## Security

Passwords are stored only as a hash (argon2id). Sessions can be ended at any time and expire on their own. Traffic is encrypted. If an incident happens that could cause relevant risk, we notify you and the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

## Minors

Contraponha is for people aged 16 or older.

## Changes to this policy

When the policy changes in a relevant way, we notify you in advance and ask you to accept the new version. Earlier versions remain available on request.
